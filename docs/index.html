Your snippets, organized and one keystroke away

Snippet Organizer is a browser extension that helps you save, categorize, search, and reuse text snippets — with an optional, manual Google Drive backup that only you control.

What Snippet Organizer does

It runs entirely in your browser and is built around one job: keep the text you reuse — replies, code fragments, templates, links — tidy and instantly available.

📝

Snippets

Create, edit, and organize text snippets with titles, content, categories, favorites, and timestamps. Deleted snippets are soft-deleted so restores can detect conflicts safely.

🗂️

Categories

Group snippets into color-coded categories so related items stay together at a glance.

🔍

Quick search & one-click copy

Find any snippet instantly and copy it to your clipboard with a single click or shortcut.

🪟

Popup & floating window

Open the docked toolbar popup or a detached floating window; toggle with Ctrl+Shift+X.

⚙️

Settings you control

Preferences live on your device, and connecting Google is always an explicit choice you make in Settings.

☁️

Optional Google Drive backup

Create, list, restore, and delete backup snapshots in your own Drive — manually, whenever you choose.

The optional Google Drive backup model

Connecting your Google Account is optional. The extension works fully without it, and no Google permission is requested until you connect in Settings.

Limited backup-file access

When connected, Snippet Organizer uses the drive.appdata permission, which limits access to a single hidden application folder (appDataFolder) in your own Google Drive. It cannot browse, read, or modify any of your other Drive files or folders. Backup snapshots are JSON files created there on your explicit action; you can restore them, list them, or delete them at any time from the extension's Settings page.

There is no automatic sync: backups happen only when you trigger them, and nothing runs in the background.
